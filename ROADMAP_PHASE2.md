# 🗺️ Razel Tech Photo Booth Pro — Phase 2 Development Roadmap

This document outlines the planned feature expansions, technical enhancements, and UX roadmap for **Phase 2** of Razel Tech Photo Booth Pro.

---

## 🌟 Priority 1: Individual / Mixed Per-Frame Shaders & Filters

### **Concept**
Currently, analogue film shaders (Silver B&W, Polaroid, Sepia, Kodachrome) apply uniformly across all photo slots in a strip. In Phase 2, users will be able to assign **individual filters per frame**:

- **Mixed Filter Strips**:
  - `Frame #1`: 🎞️ Silver Gelatin Vintage B&W
  - `Frame #2`: 📸 Polaroid SX-70 Instant
  - `Frame #3`: ☀️ 1970s Warm Kodachrome
  - `Frame #4`: 🌾 Vintage Sepia Nostalgia
- **Per-Frame Retake Selector**: When hovering over a single frame slot to click `🔄 Retake`, users can change that individual photo's filter independently without altering the rest of the strip.

---

## 🎞️ Priority 2: Enhanced Animated GIF & Motion Reel Engine

- **High-Definition WebM / MP4 Video Loop Exporter**.
- **Adjustable Frame Delay Slider** (`200ms` fast flash flip-book to `1000ms` slow motion loop).
- **Progressive Strip Assembly Motion Reel** (showing photos appearing frame-by-frame on card stock).

---

## 📱 Priority 3: Instant QR Code Mobile Kiosk Download

- **Kiosk Event Mode**: Generate a dynamic QR code on the studio display after shooting.
- **Instant Smartphone Scan**: Event guests at weddings or birthday booths can scan the QR code with their mobile phone camera to instantly view and download their digital photo strip.

---

## 🖼️ Priority 4: Custom Event Logo PNG Overlay

- **Sponsor & Monogram Overlay**: Allow event hosts to upload a transparent PNG logo (e.g. Wedding Monograms, Corporate Event Logos) that automatically stamps on the bottom or top of the photo strip.

---

## 🔊 Priority 5: Spoken Voice Countdown Sound Effects

- **Audio Cues**: Optional spoken voice cues (*"3... 2... 1... Say Cheese! 📸"*) alongside the mechanical DSLR shutter thump for an interactive kiosk experience.

---

## 🖨️ Priority 6: 2x6" Dual-Cut DNP Thermal Print Mode

- **Printer Preset**: Preset layout for commercial thermal event printers (DNP DS620, Fujifilm, HiTi) that print two side-by-side 2x6" strips on a single 4x6" print sheet.
